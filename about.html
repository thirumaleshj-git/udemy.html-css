Privacy

We are big believers in privacy rights.

We do not share or sell your data, we do not have advertisements, we do not track individual user movements on our site, and we do not participate in dark patterns.

We do let you export your data at any time, we do remove your data completely from our servers if you delete your account, and we do certify through the Data Privacy Framework.
